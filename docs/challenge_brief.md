# Challenge Brief

Investigate a possible enterprise compromise at Asterion Dynamics following:
- unexpected access to M&A and board-prep documents
- suspicious cloud role use tied to CI/CD
- anomalous Kubernetes workload execution
- potential release-pipeline tampering concerns
- unusual MFA prompts
- DLP alerts involving exports and sanctioned SaaS shares

Constraints:
- do not assume every artifact is causally related
- do not assume every alert is malicious
- do not assume signing infrastructure was actually used just because it was reachable
- mixed time zones and drift exist
